What was the starting point?
 
Current state: the information flow was the bottleneck
Over-processing. 387 sheets in March, one per order, ~2,900 hand entries.
Data in ~28 disconnected sources. Paper, spreadsheets and memory. No single figure the cell agreed on.
Double handling. The hour-by-hour board was written by hand, then typed again.
Waiting. The shift was consolidated by hand and existed only the next morning. One order sat 4,162 h in a stage before anyone noticed.
Traceability by archaeology. A complaint meant digging through paper, so containment covered the whole delivery instead of the affected lot.
